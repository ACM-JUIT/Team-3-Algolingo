Lore & Legend

For centuries, the Python Tower stood as the ultimate challenge for aspiring programmers. Inside its cold, stone-brick walls reside gatekeepers who test travelers' command of variables, structures, and operators.

To ascend, a Hero Coder must defeat these manifestation beasts. When the logic is faulty, the beasts strike back, draining the hero's energy (HP). Only true commands can defeat the beasts, allowing entry to higher floors.

Do you have what it takes to optimize your logic and reach the summit?
